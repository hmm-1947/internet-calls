Privacy Policy – LailaNow

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

At LailaNow, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website, mobile application, or related services ("Platform").

By accessing or using LailaNow, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

1. About LailaNow

LailaNow is a platform that connects users with trained listeners for supportive conversations. LailaNow is not a medical, psychological, psychiatric, crisis intervention, or emergency service. Our listeners are not licensed therapists, psychologists, psychiatrists, or healthcare professionals unless expressly stated.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our services safely and efficiently, we may collect the following categories of information.

A. Personal Information

When you create an account or use our services, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Profile photograph (optional)
  • Date of birth or age
  • Gender (optional)
  • City or country
  • Billing and payment information (processed securely through payment partners)

B. Device Information

We may automatically collect:

  • Device model
  • Operating system
  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Browser information
  • App version
  • Crash reports
  • Diagnostic information

C. Usage Information

We may collect information about how you use LailaNow, including:

  • Login activity
  • Features used
  • Session duration
  • Search history within the app
  • Interaction with listeners
  • Preferences and settings

D. Permission-Based Information

With your permission, LailaNow may access:

  • Device storage
  • Photos or media (if you upload files)
  • Notifications
  • Contacts (only if you explicitly grant access)
  • Camera or microphone (where applicable)
  • Approximate or precise location (only if required for specific features)

You may revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

3. Why We Collect Information

We collect your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide our services
  • Connect you with listeners
  • Process payments
  • Improve platform performance
  • Personalize your experience
  • Send important service notifications
  • Respond to support requests
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam, and misuse
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect the safety and security of users

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the Platform
  • Verify user identity
  • Improve service quality
  • Analyze usage trends
  • Detect technical issues
  • Enhance safety measures
  • Communicate updates regarding your account
  • Enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With payment processors for secure transactions
  • With cloud hosting or technology providers that help operate our services
  • When required by applicable law, court order, or government authority
  • To investigate fraud, abuse, illegal activities, or security threats
  • During a merger, acquisition, or business restructuring (subject to appropriate safeguards)

We require third-party service providers to protect your information in accordance with applicable laws.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Meet legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Prevent fraud
  • Enforce our agreements

After the retention period expires, information will be securely deleted or anonymized where reasonably practicable.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

LailaNow may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you signed in
  • Improve performance
  • Remember preferences
  • Analyze usage
  • Enhance user experience

You may disable cookies through your browser settings, although certain features may not function properly.

9. External Links

Our Platform may contain links to third-party websites or services.

LailaNow does not control these websites and is not responsible for their privacy practices, content, or security. Users should review the privacy policies of any third-party services they visit.

10. User Responsibilities

To help maintain a safe environment, users agree that they will not:

  • Share confidential personal information with other users or listeners
  • Share passwords, banking information, or government identification documents
  • Upload unlawful, abusive, defamatory, threatening, or offensive content
  • Share sexually explicit or adult content
  • Engage in harassment or hate speech

  • Use the Platform for illegal activities

LailaNow is intended only for users who meet the minimum legal age required under applicable laws.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate legal authorization. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Subject to applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information

  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw previously given consent
  • Object to certain processing activities
  • Request a copy of your personal information
  • Lodge a complaint with an appropriate regulatory authority

We will respond to legitimate requests within a reasonable timeframe.
