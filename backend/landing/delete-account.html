Account & Data

Delete Your MindiCo Account

You can request deletion of your MindiCo account and associated personal data at any time. Follow the instructions below to submit your request.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your MindiCo account.
  2. Use the subject line Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the name and email address or mobile number associated with your account.
  4. We may verify the request before processing it to protect your account and personal information.

MindiCo Account Deletion Support

Email: MindiCoapp@gmail.com

Request Account Deletion

What will be deleted?

Subject to applicable legal and security requirements, we will delete personal information associated with your account, including information that is no longer required to provide the service.

  • Account and profile information
  • Name, email address and mobile number associated with the account
  • Profile photograph, where applicable
  • Age/date-of-birth and optional profile information
  • Conversation or listener-interaction information associated with the account, where applicable
  • Uploaded media or other account-associated information, where applicable

Information that may be retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required by applicable law, to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, enforce agreements, or protect the security of MindiCo and its users.

Where information is required to be retained, it will be kept only for the period reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and will be securely deleted or anonymized when no longer required, where reasonably practicable.

Processing of your request

We will review legitimate deletion requests and respond within a reasonable timeframe in accordance with applicable laws and our privacy practices.

If additional information is required to verify that you own the account, we may request it before completing the deletion request.

Important: Deleting your account may permanently remove access to your account and associated services. Account deletion may not be reversible once completed.
