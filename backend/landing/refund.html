Refund Policy – LailaNow

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

At LailaNow, we believe in transparency and fairness. This Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which we may issue refunds for payments made on our Platform.

By using LailaNow, you accept this Refund Policy. Please read it carefully before making any purchase.

1. No Refunds for Services Rendered

LailaNow connects users with trained listeners for supportive conversations. Payments made for listening sessions, subscriptions, or any other service are non-refundable once the service has been delivered or the session has started.

We do not offer refunds for:

  • Completed listening sessions
  • Unused session minutes (unless covered by a specific subscription plan)
  • Subscription fees for the remainder of the billing period after cancellation
  • User dissatisfaction with a listener, conversation style, or advice
  • Any subjective outcome or feeling of the session

LailaNow is not a medical or therapeutic service – we are a platform for emotional support and conversation. Refunds are not provided based on the perceived quality or outcome of a conversation.

2. Refunds for Genuine Technical Issues

We understand that technical glitches can happen. If you encounter a genuine, verifiable technical issue that prevents you from accessing or using a paid service you have purchased, we may issue a partial or full refund at our discretion.

Examples of eligible technical issues include:

  • Platform outage or downtime during your scheduled session
  • Billing errors resulting in duplicate charges or incorrect amounts
  • Payment deducted but service not delivered due to a system error
  • Persistent app crashes or login failures that prevent access

To request a refund for a technical issue, you must:

  • Provide your registered email and payment details
  • Describe the technical issue in detail
  • Include screenshots, logs, or timestamps (if available)
  • Submit your request within 7 calendar days from the date of the issue

3. How to Request a Refund

All refund requests must be submitted via email to:

Refund Request
Email: lailanowapp@gmail.com

We will review your request and respond within 7 business days. If approved, refunds will be processed through the original payment method within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or payment gateway.

4. Refund Processing

Refunds are processed using the same payment method you used to make the purchase. Please note that:

  • Processing times may vary depending on your bank, card issuer, or payment provider
  • Any payment gateway or transaction fees are non-refundable
  • We reserve the right to ask for additional information to verify your identity and purchase

If you cancel a subscription, you will not be charged for the next billing cycle. However, no refunds are provided for the current paid period.

5. Exceptions

No refunds will be issued for:

  • Issues caused by your own device, network, or user error
  • Third-party payment gateway declines or errors
  • Requests submitted after the 7-day window
  • Failure to cancel a subscription before the renewal date
  • Promotional offers, discounts, or gift vouchers
  • Any service that was delivered as described

6. Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback with your bank or payment provider before contacting us, we reserve the right to suspend or terminate your account. We encourage you to contact us directly so we can resolve any issue promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

LailaNow may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the Platform after changes are made constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Refund Policy or need assistance with a refund request, please reach out to us:

LailaNow
Email: lailanowapp@gmail.com

We are here to help and will do our best to address your concerns fairly.
